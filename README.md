# LifecycleLoop Live

Internal employee communication program. Employers use LifecycleLoop Live to send real-time text updates to their own enrolled employees at each stage of the employee lifecycle: onboarding steps, training reminders, schedule and shift changes, benefits enrollment deadlines, workplace announcements, and one-time verification codes to confirm your mobile number. Messages go only to current employees of a participating employer who enroll with their company ID.

Live at: https://lifecyclelooplive.com
